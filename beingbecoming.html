    Being & Becoming

     

    Video / Choreography / Sound

     

    Freya Björg Olafson

     

    Performers: Spenser Theberge, Arika Yamada, Brittany Davis, Lindsay Parker, Jordan McHenry

     

    Mentor: Helene Blackburn

     

    18 minute performance

     

     

     

    Past Presentations:

    Springboard Danse Montréal

    final presentation at Usine C - Centre Multi-Disciplinaire (Montréal)  2007

     

    During The Springboard Danse Montreal Project in July 2007 I had the opportunity to create an installation performance work under the mentorship of artistic director/ choreographer of Cas Public, Helene Blackburn. The Springboard Danse Project concluded with a performance of the new works of four selected emerging choreographers (including myself) alongside those of participating senior choreographers Christopher House, Helene Blackburn, Roger Sinha, and Melanie Demers in Montreal at Usine C on July 13th, 2007.

     

    In Being and Becoming' I was interested physical and emotional transformations. Over 2005 - 2006 years my work enaged with notions of identity, stemming from my MFA in New Media research into the areas of cultural and gender theory. With five dancers I focused on the manifestations of internal transformation through facial and physical expression. 'Being and Becoming' is 15 minutes in length and is performed in tandem with edited video that is projected on the wall as well as played on two television monitors. This creation is a continuation of my interest in refining the integration of video and performance. I choreographed the new work within the three weeks at Springboard as well as shot and edited video and manipulated sound material which I had been working with since December 2006. 

     

    This opportunity at SpringBoard Danse was generously supported by:

    The Canada Council for The Arts

    &   Manitoba Arts Council
